## Brand Strategy

### Positioning
Marina Dorcol is positioned as a premium riverside residential development in Belgrade's historic Dorcol neighborhood. The brand promise centers on reconnecting urban living with nature -- the river, cityscape, and greenery -- through thoughtfully designed apartments.

### Brand Claim
> "Welcome on board."

### Purpose Statement
The brand seeks to "harness the river, the cityscape and the greenery that surround us, and offer brilliantly designed apartments that allow for better living." The messaging emphasizes small apartment buildings set to respect sunlight, river views, green loggias, and a countryside feel within the city.

### Key Messaging Pillars
- **River/waterfront living** -- marina lifestyle, nautical heritage of the location
- **Nature integration** -- greenery, loggias, countryside feeling within the city
- **Considered design** -- sunlight-aware layouts, curtain windows, architectural quality
- **Belonging** -- "a place you love coming home to"

### Document Structure
1. **Our Voice** (pp. 3--5): Claim and purpose
2. **Brand Elements** (pp. 6--29): Brand marks, colors, typography, photography
3. **Grids & Layouts** (pp. 30--33): Grid systems and layout examples
4. **Applications** (pp. 34--52): Print, digital, signage, merchandise

## Logo Guidelines

### Logo System Components
The identity consists of three distinct elements that can be used independently:

1. **Full Logo (Lock-up)** -- Symbol above wordmark "MARINA DORCOL" (pp. 8--9)
2. **Wordmark only** -- "MARINA DORCOL" in all-caps, wide letter-spacing (pp. 11--12)
3. **Symbol only** -- Stylized "M" enclosed in a circle with an open top arc (pp. 13--14)

### Symbol Description
The symbol is a geometric "M" inside a circle. The circle features a distinctive open arc at the top, giving it a subtle crown or wave-like quality. The "M" is constructed with clean, stencil-influenced strokes -- the center apex of the M rises to nearly touch the top arc of the circle. The overall effect is nautical/compass-like while remaining minimal.

### Symbol Geometry (p. 15)
Detailed construction grid provided showing the symbol built on a square grid with diagonal construction lines. The circle is derived from precise geometric relationships within the grid.

### Safety Area (p. 10)
A defined clear space surrounds the logo. Specific measurements shown relative to the symbol height.

### Logo Sizes (p. 16)
Three size variants demonstrated: small symbol-only usage, medium wordmark-only usage, and large full lock-up. The system is designed to scale from favicon/app-icon size up to large-format signage.

### Color Versions
- **Positive**: Marina Blue (#004C97) on white
- **Negative**: White on Marina Blue background
- Both demonstrated for full logo, wordmark, and symbol independently

### Wordmark Details
The wordmark uses all-uppercase characters with generous letter-spacing. The "C" in "DORCOL" features the Serbian hacek accent (C with dot above). The typeface appears to be based on the primary brand font Cera Stencil Pro, giving the wordmark a distinctive stencil-cut character.

## Typography

### Primary Typeface: Cera Stencil Pro
- **Foundry**: Typemates (typemates.com)
- **Weights used**: Light, Regular, Medium
- **Role**: Headings, titles, important information, display text
- **Character**: Asymmetric stencilling creates dynamic tension, emphasizing handwritten origins. Clean and modern with distinctive stencil cuts. Supports multiple languages including Serbian (with diacritical characters).

### Secondary Typeface: Cera Pro
- **Foundry**: Typemates (typemates.com)
- **Weights used**: Light, Regular, Medium
- **Role**: Body copy, secondary text
- **Character**: Six weights (Thin to Black) with matching italics sloped at 10 degrees. The "o" remains a perfect circle even in italic. Designed for interfaces and corporate design.

## Supporting Graphics

### Grid System (pp. 31--33)
Three distinct grid structures are provided:
1. **6-column equal grid** -- uniform square modules
2. **6-column equal grid variant** -- slightly adjusted proportions
3. **3-column asymmetric grid** -- wider modules for editorial/content layouts

Layout examples (p. 33) show how grids are applied in practice, combining photography with large typographic headlines and the brand tagline "Settle down in Belgrade's new riverside district."

### Duotone Image Treatment (p. 47)
Display ads use a distinctive duotone photographic treatment where images are rendered in shades of Marina Blue and white, creating a monochromatic, high-contrast look. This is a consistent visual device across digital advertising.

### Stencil Character
The stencil cuts in the primary typeface serve as an implicit graphic motif -- the breaks in letter strokes create a recognizable visual rhythm that carries across all branded materials without needing additional decorative elements.

## Outliers / Notable

1. **Stencil typeface as identity anchor**: The choice of Cera Stencil Pro as the primary display face is distinctive and unusual for real estate. The stencil cuts create a strong visual signature without relying on additional graphic devices. This is a sophisticated design decision that gives the brand instant recognition.

2. **Placeholder body text in letterhead**: The letterhead example (p. 35) uses an amusing lorem-ipsum-style passage about mermaids and naturalist Carl Linnaeus rather than project-specific copy. This suggests the guidelines were produced with temporary content -- worth noting for reproduction.

3. **Token-based color naming**: The color system uses a programmatic naming convention (Family.Weight) that maps cleanly to design tokens / CSS variables. This is forward-thinking and suggests the guidelines were built with digital implementation in mind.

4. **Duotone image treatment**: The blue monochrome photographic treatment in digital ads is a strong differentiator but is not extensively documented as a technique -- it appears only by example on the display ads page rather than being codified as a formal rule.

5. **Serbian diacritical handling**: The brand name uses the proper Serbian character "C" (C with overdot) in "Dorcol," and the typography section explicitly demonstrates Serbian-specific characters through a local pangram. Full Cyrillic/Latin support is implied but not separately detailed.

6. **Landscape presentation format**: The 1920x1080 format (matching HD screen resolution) makes this document ideal for screen presentation but potentially awkward for print reference. No A4/letter format version appears to exist.

7. **No explicit "don'ts" section**: Unlike many brand guidelines, this document does not include a logo misuse page or color restriction rules. The guidelines are presented entirely through positive examples.

8. **Historic photography category**: The inclusion of black-and-white archival photos as a formal photography category is distinctive and ties the brand to place-specific heritage -- uncommon for new-build developments.

## Quality Score

**4 / 5**

### Justification
- **Strengths**: Exceptionally clean design system with strong internal consistency. The color palette is well-structured with token-ready naming. Typography hierarchy is thorough with specific size/leading values. The logo system (lock-up, wordmark, symbol) is complete with positive/negative versions, geometry, safety area, and sizing. Photography direction is clearly categorized. Application examples are extensive, spanning print, digital, signage, and merchandise across 18 pages.
- **Weaknesses**: No logo misuse / "don'ts" section. The duotone photography treatment is shown but not formally documented as a technique. No explicit guidance on color combinations or accessibility contrast ratios. The Beige.ExtraLight hex value appears to contain a data error (#004C97 repeated instead of the actual light beige). Placeholder text in the letterhead example. No explicit icon set beyond navigation arrows.
- **Overall**: A polished, professional document that covers all essential brand identity elements with strong visual craft. The minor gaps (misuse rules, formal duotone spec) keep it from a perfect score, but it is immediately usable for implementation.
