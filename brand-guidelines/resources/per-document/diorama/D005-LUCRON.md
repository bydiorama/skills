## Logo Guidelines

### Form
The logotype is a **lowercase wordmark** ("lucron.") set in the corporate typeface **Karbon Bold**, terminated by a characteristic period/dot after the word. There is no icon, monogram, or symbol -- the dot after "lucron" serves as the sole distinguishing mark.

### Safety zone
Defined by the dimensions of the letter **"o"** in the wordmark. The clear space equal to the height/width of the "o" must be maintained on all sides. No design elements may encroach on this area.

### Color variants
Only two versions are permitted:
- **100% black** on light backgrounds
- **100% white** (reversed) on dark backgrounds

No other color treatments are allowed.

### Forbidden uses (9 examples shown)
1. Do not stretch horizontally
2. Do not stretch vertically
3. Do not rotate
4. Do not change color (e.g., red, blue)
5. Do not use an outlined version
6. Do not place inside shapes (circle, rectangle)
7. Do not place inside a circle
8. Do not use on low-contrast solid backgrounds (e.g., dark gray on black)
9. Do not use on low-contrast photographic backgrounds

## Typography

### Primary typeface -- Karbon
- **Karbon Regular** -- body text, subheadlines
- **Karbon Bold** -- headlines, superheadlines
- **Karbon Light** and **Karbon Medium** -- available for more sophisticated hierarchy
- The typeface includes both lining and oldstyle figures
- Classification: geometric sans-serif (by Bold Monday, originally designed by Paul Renner influences)

### Secondary typeface -- Maven Pro
- **Maven Pro Regular** and **Maven Pro Bold**
- Used only as a fallback when Karbon is unavailable
- Same hierarchy rules apply as with Karbon
- Classification: open-source geometric sans-serif (Google Fonts)

## Supporting Graphics

### The dot pattern
The signature graphic device is the **"lucron. dot pattern"** -- a modular system of grid-based circular dots arranged in varying densities to create organic, textured areas. The system works as follows:

- Six base modules are shown: dense grid, scattered grid, sparse grid, vertical bar grid, diagonal arrangement, and horizontal bar grid
- Modules are combined to build larger compositions
- Full-page compositions are demonstrated in both light (off-white/gray dots on white) and dark (white/gray dots on black) variants
- Select dots within the pattern are colored using the four secondary palette colors, creating subtle accent points within the predominantly monochrome grid
- The pattern appears on the cover page, roll-up banner, and presentation slides

### Icon sets
Two custom icon styles are defined:
1. **Outlined** -- line-based icons with dot accents at endpoints and intersections (smiley, envelope, euro symbol, parking, notification bell, info, thumbs up, share/arrow, building). The dots at structural points echo the brand's dot motif.
2. **Dotted** -- the same icon subjects rendered entirely from dots, reinforcing the dot pattern language (smiley, frown, euro, video/film, recycle, music, globe, home, arrow).

## Outliers / Notable

1. **WIP status** -- The cover explicitly states "Work in progress." The TOC promises 29 pages of content but the PDF contains only 24 physical pages, with at least 5 application sections missing (business card, letterhead, office articles, annual report, mail signature, merch).
2. **Typo in section title** -- The typography chapter divider is spelled "Typograhpy" (transposed "h" and "p") on page 8.
3. **Typo in CMYK value** -- Sky blue lists "C 440" which should be "C 44."
4. **Typo in black hex** -- The primary black is listed as "#00000" (5 digits) rather than "#000000."
5. **Dot as brand element** -- The trailing period in "lucron." is not just punctuation; it is integral to the brand identity and connects to the dot pattern system and the dotted icon set. This creates a cohesive visual thread across the entire identity.
6. **Extreme minimalism** -- The brand system is deliberately constrained: wordmark-only logo, black-and-white primary palette, a single typeface family. The secondary palette and dot pattern add warmth and texture without undermining the monochrome foundation.
7. **Parent company reference** -- Lucron is identified as part of **iko real estate** group from Luxembourg, though this is only mentioned in body copy, not in any brand architecture diagram.
8. **No digital guidelines** -- No web, app, social media, or responsive specifications are provided.

However, it is explicitly unfinished -- multiple promised application sections are absent, there are no photography guidelines, no tone-of-voice section, no digital specifications, and several typos remain (section title, hex value, CMYK value). As a WIP draft it shows strong design direction; as a deliverable brand guidelines document it is incomplete.
