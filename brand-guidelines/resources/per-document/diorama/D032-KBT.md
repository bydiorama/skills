## Brand Strategy

- **Concept:** Focus on "stone transformation" -- the brand identity mirrors the process of shaping and refining stone. This is expressed through a unique typeface that "changes shape" and geometric icons (squares, circles, diamonds with carved-out interiors).
- **Brand board:** Combines stone texture photography, bold geometric iconography, the KBT italic logotype, and a palette inspired by natural stone colors with modern design accents.
- **Positioning:** Industrial/craft brand with strong visual identity. The website kamen.sk ("kamen" = "stone" in Slovak) reinforces the core material identity.
- **Chapters:** Brand Introduction (p.02) and Concept (p.03-04) precede visual identity rules -- indicating some strategic framing.

## Logo Guidelines

## Typography

- **Primary font:** Saans -- clean, modern sans-serif used for body text and the "kamen.sk" domain name treatment. Ensures readability and accessibility.
- **Secondary / display font:** KBT Blox -- a bespoke, custom-made variable monospace font. Contains only capital letters "K", "B", "T" and numbers (0-9) in variable weights. Designed to symbolize the stone transformation process.
- **KBT Blox variants (6 total):**
  - **KBT Block** -- Heaviest weight; letterforms are nearly solid rectangles. Represents raw, unprocessed stone.
  - **KBT Raw** -- Medium weight italic; partially shaped.
  - **KBT Sculpt** -- Lightest/most refined weight; clean, finished letterforms. Represents the final processed product.
  - **KBT Block Slant** -- Slanted version of Block.
  - **KBT Raw Slant** -- Slanted version of Raw.
  - **KBT Sculpt Slant** -- Slanted version of Sculpt.
- **Metaphor:** The Block-to-Raw-to-Sculpt progression mirrors the physical stone processing journey -- from raw quarried block to refined building material. This is the central conceptual device of the brand identity.
