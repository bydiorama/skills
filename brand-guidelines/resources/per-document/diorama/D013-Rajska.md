## Brand Strategy

The identity of Rajska 5 is derived from two core brand attributes: **exclusivity** and **central yet tranquil location** combined with quality architecture. The visual identity targets a more demanding (premium) clientele through visual purity and cleanliness.

- **Positioning:** "Exkluzivne byvanie v centre diania" (Exclusive living in the center of activity)
- **Brand Pillars:** Exclusivity, central location, architectural quality, tranquility
- **Project Spec:** 12 apartments, 6 floors, units from 50 m2
- The brand is a single-project identity for a boutique residential building, not a corporate umbrella brand. The entire visual system is subordinate to the building's address as its name.

No formal brand values matrix or mission/vision statements are included. Strategy is implied through design rationale descriptions on individual pages.

## Logo Guidelines

### Primary Logotype (pp. 3-5)

- **Form:** Custom wordmark reading "RAJSKA 5" set in **Macan Stencil** typeface
- **Stencil Treatment:** The logotype uses characteristic stencil breaks/gaps in the letterforms, giving it a distinctive architectural quality. Letters have deliberate interruptions -- portions of strokes are removed, creating a refined industrial aesthetic
- **Case:** All uppercase
- **Versions:**
  - **Primary (dark):** White logotype on black background (p. 3)
  - **Inverse (light):** Black logotype on white background (p. 4)
- **Clear Space (Ochranna zona):** Defined by the width and height of the numeral "5" applied equally on all four sides of the logotype (p. 5)

### Symbol -- Number 5 (pp. 6-7)

- The numeral **"5"** functions as a standalone brand symbol/icon
- Used where full logotype is impractical due to technical limitations or is redundant
- Rendered in the same Macan Stencil typeface with characteristic stencil breaks
- **Clear Space for Symbol:** Defined by the top stroke element of the "5" numeral, applied equally on all sides (p. 7)

### Logo Color Usage (p. 10)

Four approved combinations:
1. Black logotype on white background (primary)
2. Black logotype on brown/taupe background
3. White logotype on black background
4. Brown/taupe logotype on black background

## Typography

### Primary Typeface (p. 12)

- **Font:** PP Neue Montreal Medium
- **Designer:** Mathieu Desjardins
- **Foundry:** Pangram Pangram
- **Rationale:** Inspired by the legendary Helvetica; captures the urban character of the project. Tighter spacing between characters creates its own dynamic and rhythm without using decorative elements. Reflects the project's architecture
- **Character Set:** Full Latin alphabet, numerals, special characters shown

### Secondary Typeface (p. 13)

- **Font:** Macan Stencil
- **Foundry:** Tight Type
- **Role:** Used in the logo and headings as an alternative/display face
- **Rationale:** Uses stencil elements giving it an industrial character, but the choice of break points and form makes it elegant and sophisticated, serving as the primary differentiating element of the brand communication
- **Character Set:** Full Latin alphabet, numerals, special characters shown

### Type Hierarchy (p. 14)

| Level | Font | Size | Tracking | Line Height |

### Color Usage Notes

- White is the **primary carrier color** of the entire identity
- Black is used **primarily for text**
- Brown/taupe serves for **emphasis and highlighting** information that warrants communicative weight
- The brown/taupe is extensively used as a background color throughout the guidelines themselves, giving the document a warm, premium feel

A custom icon set rendered in a style consistent with the Macan Stencil typeface -- line-based with characteristic stencil breaks/gaps:

**Directional icons (p. 16, top row):**
- Up arrow, Down arrow, Right arrow, Diagonal up-left arrow, Diagonal up-right arrow, Checkmark, X/Close

## Outliers / Notable

1. **Stencil typeface as core differentiator:** The entire identity revolves around Macan Stencil's characteristic gaps. This is unusual for real estate branding, which typically uses serif or clean sans-serif fonts. The stencil treatment references industrial/architectural construction processes (stenciling on building materials) while remaining elegant

2. **Extremely restrained color palette:** Only three colors (white, black, brown/taupe) with no accent or secondary colors at all. This is more minimal than most residential property brands

3. **"5" as standalone symbol:** The numeral from the street address becomes the brand icon -- a clever solution that ties the identity directly to the physical location. The "5" in Macan Stencil is highly distinctive and recognizable

4. **Custom pictogram system:** The icons maintain stencil breaks consistent with the typeface, which is an unusually thorough level of craft for a single-building residential project

5. **No photography guidelines:** Despite using photography extensively in applications, there are no formal photo direction rules (mood, treatment, composition guidelines)

6. **No "don'ts" section:** The manual does not include common misuse examples or prohibited logo treatments

7. **Wayfinding integration:** The brand extends into physical building navigation, which shows the identity was conceived holistically as both a marketing and operational system

8. **Document language entirely in Slovak:** No English translation or parallel text provided
