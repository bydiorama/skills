## Brand Strategy

- **Claim / Tagline:** "Mame!" (Slovak for "We have it!") -- a one-word promise that REMPO stocks everything a customer needs in one place, without delay.
- **Brand promise:** Comprehensive supply of protective, work and specialist equipment; 65+ years of market presence.
- **Positioning:** Market leader in occupational safety and workwear distribution in Slovakia; positioned as a reliable, no-nonsense partner for tradespeople, private companies and public-sector organisations.
- **Claim usage pattern:** The claim is designed as a call-and-response device -- a question about a product/service followed by "Mame!" (e.g. "Pracovne odevy? Mame!", "Bezpecnostnu obuv? Mame!", "Aj nemozne? Mame!"). This makes it versatile for campaign variations.
- **Company structure:** Two divisions -- key-account products & services, and retail.
- **Headquarters:** Stara Vajnorska 19, 832 45 Bratislava, Slovakia.

## Logo Guidelines

- **Logo type:** Wordmark ("REMPO") enclosed in a distinctive rounded-rectangle frame/border.
- **Color rules:**
  - On dark backgrounds: always white.
  - On light backgrounds: always primary Rempo Blue (#004FB5), where reproduction technology allows.
- **Recommended sizes:** Defined at 90 mm, 60 mm, 30 mm, 20 mm, and 16 px. At smaller sizes the border stroke weight should be proportionally increased for legibility.
- **Favicon / extreme small sizes:** The letter "R" may be extracted from the logo and used as a standalone symbol.
- **Clear space (ochranna zona):** Defined by the height of the logo (dimension "x") applied equally on all four sides.
- **No alternate versions shown** (no stacked/horizontal variants described beyond the single wordmark form).

## Typography

- **Primary typeface:** Matter (by Displaay Type, Czech foundry).
- **Weights used:** SemiBold and Regular only.
- **Design rationale:** Technical enough for REMPO's industrial audience, yet approachable for commercial communication.
- **Usage rules:**
  - SemiBold: claim, headings, product names, prices, dominant text.
  - Regular: body copy, longer-form text.
- **Typographic hierarchy (size / line-height ratios):**
  - Display 1: 72/80
  - Heading 1 / Page Title: 64/72
  - Heading 3 / Section Title: 48/56
  - Heading 4 / Section Subtitle: 40/48
  - Heading 5 / Subtitle: 32/40
  - Heading 6 / Large Copy: 24/32
  - Text M / Standard Copy: 16/24
  - Text S / Small Copy: 14/18
  - Overline: 13/16
- **Note:** Sizes are expressed as ratios, not fixed px/pt values -- they should be adapted to the format at hand.

## Supporting Graphics

### Graphic Elements (Graficke prvky)

- **Rounded-corner shapes:** Four variants of soft, organic rounded-rectangle shapes derived from the logo's border motif (pp. 34).
- **Placement rule:** Two of the rounded corners of any graphic element must always sit on the canvas/surface edge -- the shape should bleed off the format (p. 35).
- **Color application:** Graphic elements use colors from both primary and secondary palettes; examples show various color combinations (pp. 37-38).

### Framing Device (Oramovanie)

- A signature brand device derived from the logo's border line.
- The border line is deformed -- its stroke weight and corner radii are manipulated to create a distinctive, recognizable visual element.
- Can be rendered in single or combined colors, split by surface area, or filled with photography.
- May overlap other objects or extend beyond the format edge.

### Iconography (Ikonografia)

- Custom line-icon set presented (p. 30) covering product categories (protective clothing, safety equipment, etc.).
- Icons use a consistent thin-line style with rounded terminals, matching the brand's rounded visual language.
- Icons can be paired with labels (e.g. "Ochranne odevy" = Protective clothing) (p. 31).

### Surface Division (Delenie plochy)

- Two-color surface splits using Rempo Blue and Rempo Dark Blue.
- Default ratio: 1:1 (vertical or horizontal).
- Alternative ratios allowed: 2:1, 3:2, 4:2, etc.
- **Rule:** The larger area always uses Rempo Blue; the smaller uses Rempo Dark Blue.
- Demonstrated for both A-format and 16:9 format.

## Tone of Voice

- **Three defining attributes:** Human, direct, empathetic ("Ludsky, priamy, empaticky").
- **Language approach:** Uses the vocabulary their customers understand -- avoids marketing jargon and cliches.
- **Directness:** Respects customers' time and attention; communicates without unnecessary filler ("omacky" = waffle).
- **Empathy:** Acknowledges that running a business is hard; aims to make it easier for their customers.

## Outliers / Notable

1. **Language:** Entire manual is in Slovak, not English -- somewhat unusual for a brand manual in this format. No bilingual version provided.
2. **Claim as structural device:** The "Mame!" tagline is unusually central to the identity -- it functions not just as a tagline but as a repeatable communication template (question + "Mame!"). This is well thought-out for campaign scalability.
3. **No photography guidelines:** For a 64-page manual, the absence of explicit photography direction (mood, lighting, composition, retouching rules) is a notable gap.
4. **Single logo form:** Only one logo variant is presented (the framed wordmark). No horizontal/vertical/stacked alternatives, no monochrome version rules beyond light/dark background, and no co-branding guidelines.
5. **Premium typeface:** Matter by Displaay is a paid, specialist typeface -- signals investment in typographic identity but may create licensing friction for partners/vendors.
6. **Strong visual system:** The rounded-border framing device and graphic elements create a cohesive, ownable visual language that ties back directly to the logo.
7. **Color hierarchy with percentages:** Explicitly defining a usage ratio (50/29/10/7/3/1) for brand colors is a useful and relatively rare inclusion.

## Quality Score

**4 / 5**

**Strengths:**
- Comprehensive color system with Pantone, CMYK, RGB, and HEX values plus a usage hierarchy.
- Well-defined typographic hierarchy with clear size/leading ratios.
- Strong, ownable visual system (framing device, rounded shapes) directly derived from the logo.
- Excellent claim strategy with built-in campaign scalability.
- Extensive real-world application examples across many media formats.

**Weaknesses:**
- No photography guidelines despite heavy use of photography in applications.
- No explicit digital/web component specifications (button styles, spacing tokens, responsive behavior).
- Only one logo variant with no co-branding, partnership, or sub-brand guidance.
- Slovak-only language limits usability for international partners or teams.
