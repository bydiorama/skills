## Brand Strategy
- **Concept:** "Hniezdo" (Nest) -- the entire brand is built around the metaphor of a bird's nest as a first home. Different bird species build different nests, just as different people need different homes at different life stages.
- **Main Claim:** "Cas na vlastne hniezdo." (Time for your own nest.)
- **Tagline Variations:** "Hniezdo je prvy domov. Pre vtaky, aj pre ludi." (A nest is the first home. For birds, and for people.)
- **Values (5):**
  1. Bezpecie (Safety) -- quality housing, modern features, peace and security
  2. Komunita (Community) -- shared park, playgrounds, spaces where neighbors meet
  3. Rovnovaha (Balance) -- proximity to both pine forests/nature and city center of Malacky
  4. Pohodlie (Comfort) -- underfloor heating, air conditioning, smart solutions, easy access to Bratislava
  5. Rozmanitost (Diversity) -- small starter apartments, family apartments, and row houses
- **Positioning:** Not premium/luxury language; positions as a thoughtful, human, nature-connected residential project. 30 minutes from Bratislava.
- **Claim Variations for campaigns:**
  - "Ked pride cas vyletiet z rodinneho hniezda."
  - "Planujete sa zahniezdit?"
  - "Nove byty prilietaju do Malaciek."
  - "Vtaci sa stahuju na juh, Vy mozete do Malaciek."

## Logo Guidelines
- **Typeface in logo:** Season Bold -- capital letters adjusted for compact impression
- **Symbol:** Combines C + A letterforms to create the silhouette of a nest
- **Construction:** Safe zone and construction based on the width of letter "i"; symbol offset is 2x the width of "i"
- **Color versions:** Works in neutral (black/white) and brand colors; defined permitted combinations for contrast
- **Minimum size:** Print 20mm, Digital 120px
- **Co-branding:** Corvus logo combined with ATOPS logo using a vertical separator line, visually balanced
- **Favicon:** Dark and light versions available; full logo and symbol-only variants
- **Restrictions:** No color combos outside permitted set, no distortion, no rotation, no unsuitable backgrounds

## Typography
- **Primary typeface:** Season Heavy -- used for headings and emphasis. Modern sans-serif with humanist character.
- **Secondary typeface:** Season Medium -- used for body and small texts. Same family, lighter weight.
- **Safe/fallback typeface:** Arial Bold and Arial Regular -- for web-safe contexts and newsletters
- **Hierarchy system (6 levels):**
  - Pre Title: 600%, line-height 90%
  - Headline Large: 300%, line-height 100%
  - Headline Small: 150%, line-height 110%
  - Copy Large: 100%, line-height 120%
  - Copy Medium: 75%, line-height 130%
  - Copy Small: 50%, line-height 140%
- All headings use Season Heavy; all body copy uses Season Medium

## Supporting Graphics
- **Nest element:** Core graphic device -- a circular/nest-shaped form used in multiple ways:
  - Full-width nest aligned to format margins
  - Centered at bottom, aligned right or left
  - Larger circle version used as cut-out within format
  - Can frame round photos, stickers, or URLs
  - Can visualize time/distance (e.g., "30 min to Bratislava" as a clock dial -- dark segment = travel time)
- **Nest pattern:** Repeating nest elements used as dividers and aesthetic elements; always bleeds off edges on both sides; minimum 2 rows, maximum 4 rows; never isolated individual elements
- **Icons:**
  - Primary set: 36x36 grid, 2-square stroke width, mix of sharp and rounded parts, simple style
  - Secondary set: Smaller UI-optimized icons for compact contexts
  - Expandable icon package for features/benefits/options
- **Time visualization:** Unique dial/clock motif using the nest circle to show travel times (5', 15', 20', 30', 40', 45', 55', 60')

## Photography
- **Color scheme:** Warm or neutral tones required
- **People:** Should appear happy and relaxed ("chill")
- **Composition:** Good composition and sufficient resolution required
- **Avoid:** Cool color schemes, overly emotive expressions, poor composition, low resolution

## Tone of Voice
- **Core principle:** Talk about home, not about housing as a product. Speak about nests, not real estate.
- **Character:** Human, calm, understandable. Simple and natural language.
- **Metaphor usage:** Gentle bird/nest metaphor rooted in nature; supports meaning without overpowering it. Not poetry, not sentiment.
- **Directness:** Say things directly. No unnecessary phrases, big words, or empty promises. If one sentence suffices, don't write three.
- **Avoid:** Hyperbole, strong adjectives ("best", "premium", "exceptional"). Let quality emerge from how and what is communicated.
- **Tone:** Friendly but not familiar. Calm but not cold. Kind but not sentimental.
- **Address:** Plural form (mnozne cislo) -- speaking to people as a group with a shared place but individual stories. Inclusive, natural, gender-neutral.
- **Brand voice summary:** "Corvus komunikuje s istotou, tichou doverou a respektom." (Corvus communicates with certainty, quiet confidence, and respect.)
