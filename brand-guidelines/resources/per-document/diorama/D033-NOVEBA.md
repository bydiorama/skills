## Brand Strategy

Noveba is positioned as the first real estate brand in the Spis region (eastern Slovakia) built on transparent pricing, fair approach, and professional standards. The brand is a subsidiary/partner of IBS Invest (track record of 60-70 units).

- **Mission:** "Noveba transforms a transaction into a partnership." Compete not on price but on transparency and professionalism.
- **Vision:** Become the partner of first choice for real estate across eastern Slovakia.
- **Values:** Trust (core), Professionalism, Transparency (published commissions, clear packages, no hidden conditions), Local Expertise (deep knowledge of the Spis market).
- **Brand Archetypes:**
  - Primary: Guardian/Caregiver -- protection, support, client peace of mind
  - Secondary: Artist/Creator -- quality output and craftsmanship
  - Tertiary: Sage/Expert -- truth, knowledge, regional expertise
- **Core Narrative:** Full-service real estate -- photography, home staging, marketing presentation, legal coordination, mortgage specialist referrals, and key handover. Price communicated upfront.

## Logo Guidelines

- **Format:** Wordmark only (no icon/symbol), using lowercase letters in Bagoss Condensed Bold.
- **Clear space:** Defined by the width of vertical strokes of the logotype. Minimum safe zone marked with "X" units.
- **Color versions:** Primary is black. White version for use on photographs or third-party contexts (e.g., sponsorships). May adopt the color scheme of the hosting identity.
- **Contrast rules:** Defined permitted color combinations ensuring sufficient contrast and readability.
- **Misuse rules:** No use on complex photographs, no rotation, no distortion, no unauthorized colors.
- **Minimum size:** 20 mm (print), 120 px (digital).
- **Co-branding:** With IBS Invest, vertically centered on text blocks; safe zone derived from spacing between logo and dividing line.
- **Favicon:** Logotype on brand yellow background.
- **Placement:** Fixed positions in top-left and bottom-left corners within a modular grid system.

## Typography

- **Primary font:** Bagoss Condensed -- Regular and Medium weights. Modern condensed sans-serif used for headings and brand typography. Described as having unique details making it more approachable and personal.
- **Secondary font:** Saans -- Regular and Semibold. For body text and longer reading.
- **Fallback:** Arial (Regular, Bold) as universal system substitute for technical compatibility.
- **Hierarchy:** All sizes designed in multiples of 8 for mathematical vertical rhythm:
  - Heading XL: 136pt / 100% line-height
  - Heading L: 80pt / 110%
  - Heading M: 56pt / 120%
  - Lead: 56pt / 120%
  - Heading S: 32pt / 130%
  - Body L: 32pt / 130%
  - Body M: 24pt / 130%
  - Body S: 16pt / 140%
- **Grid system:** Universal 12-column grid (Margin: 72, Gutter: 16) as a scalable mathematical foundation for alignment across all formats.

## Supporting Graphics

### Illustrations
- **Style:** Line-drawn, digitally sketched, black-and-white. Convey energy of naturalness and humanity. Contrast with the clean rational design and serve as a key identity element.
- **Usage rules:** Placement must respect content hierarchy; illustrations complement text without disrupting readability or dominating zones reserved for logotype and main messaging.
- **Animation:** Minimalist hand-drawn motifs in slow, smooth movement. High frame rate (24+ fps), fluid transitions (walking, waving). Reinforces cleanliness, modern sophistication, and visual comfort.

### Iconography
- **Library:** BoxIcons (publicly available library) -- geometrically clean, solid (filled) shapes with pronounced rounding.
- **Usage:** Secondary navigation and informational element. Used in boxes and with typography to categorize key property parameters and statistics (e.g., area, rooms, property type).

## Photography

### Stock Photography
- **Style:** "Warmth of home" concept. Natural lighting with warm tones (sun flares), authentic everyday life moments, relaxed composition. Evokes calm, safety, and joy of living.
- **Forbidden:** Generic stock photos with artificial feel, unnaturally high contrast, staged poses (e.g., handshake into camera), cold/sterile lighting without emotion.

### Property Photography
- **Required:** Soft warm light, correct verticals (no "falling" architecture), depth of field, context of location (greenery, urbanism), clean but lived-in interiors with design details.
- **Forbidden:** Extreme wide-angle distortion, depressive weather/dark overcast skies, inappropriate horizon height (bird's eye/frog perspective), technical deficiencies (noise, blur, over-saturated HDR).

## Tone of Voice

- **Professional, not cold** -- warm but institutional
- **Clear and direct** -- no filler, no complex sentences, no unexplained jargon
- **Confident, not arrogant** -- facts over phrases
- **Helpful and proactive** -- answer before the client asks
- **Specific** -- work with numbers, places, and time
- **Emotional awareness** -- do not suppress emotions, accept them, then bring solutions

### Language Rules
- Use consistent "we" (first person plural)
- Provide specific numbers and localities
- Explain processes step by step
- Acknowledge the emotional weight of housing decisions
- Do NOT use "I" in brand communication
- Do NOT use empty superlatives
- Do NOT use pressuring expressions
- Do NOT push luxury/exclusive positioning
- Do NOT use jargon or slang

### Example Copy
- Transparency: "We are a real estate agency with transparent commission. Clear conditions, personal approach and attention to every detail."
- Expertise: "Your home deserves more than just a listing. We guide you from valuation through legal process to key handover."
- CTAs: "Get a free consultation," "Find out the value of your property," "Schedule a meeting."

## Outliers / Notable

1. **Exceptional depth for a regional real estate brand:** 82 pages is uncommonly thorough for a local property agency. The document covers everything from brand archetypes to helmet branding.

2. **Multiples-of-8 typography system:** The type hierarchy built on mathematical multiples of 8 is a sophisticated design-systems approach rarely seen in brand guidelines of this scale.

3. **12-column grid specification:** Including margin and gutter values (72/16) provides production-ready layout guidance.

4. **Co-branding with parent company:** The IBS Invest co-branding rules acknowledge the real-world need to balance subsidiary and parent brand presence.

5. **Hand-drawn illustration as differentiator:** Using sketchy, line-drawn illustrations in contrast to the clean rational design is a deliberate tension that gives the brand personality and warmth in an industry dominated by generic photography.

6. **Full merch program:** From standard business cards to construction helmets and reflective vests -- reflects the practical reality of a property business where agents visit construction sites.

7. **Property photography guidelines:** The inclusion of specific do's and don'ts for real estate photography (verticals, HDR, wide-angle distortion) shows industry-specific awareness.

8. **Animation specifications:** Including frame rate guidance (24+ fps) and motion style for animated brand assets is forward-thinking.

9. **Pantone/RAL/CMYK/RGB/HEX:** Full color specification across all reproduction systems including RAL (relevant for architectural/construction contexts).
