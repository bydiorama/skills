## Outliers / Notable

1. **Extreme file size**: At 333 MB for 39 pages, this PDF is severely unoptimized -- likely containing uncompressed raster assets or embedded high-resolution photography. Typical for this page count would be 10-30 MB.
2. **Video branding anomaly**: Page 32 references "Cuban heritage & Slovak traditional crafts" and a handbag product ("The only handbag in the world that makes you money") -- this appears to be a portfolio company or investment example rather than WEM Private Fund's own product, suggesting the fund invests in luxury/craft consumer goods.
3. **Three-tier type system**: The PP Eiko + Sofia Pro + Georgia stack is well-considered, covering display, body, and system/fallback needs. PP Eiko is a distinctive, less common choice that gives the brand strong typographic differentiation.
4. **Pantone + RAL + CMYK + RGB + Hex**: The color specification is thorough, covering print (Pantone, CMYK), industrial/architectural (RAL), and digital (RGB, Hex) needs -- appropriate for a fund that may have physical office/event environments.
5. **Full tonal scales**: Both primary and secondary colors are provided with 5-step tint/shade ramps plus neutral companions, enabling a flexible design system.
6. **Slovak market focus**: Despite the English-language document, all applications point to the Slovak market (wemprivatefund.sk, Bratislava address, Slovak body copy, NBS regulatory references).
7. **Supporting elements are visual-only**: No textual rules for the supporting graphic system, which limits reproducibility without access to the original design files.
