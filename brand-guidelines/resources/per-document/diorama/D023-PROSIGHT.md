## Brand Strategy
- **Mission:** "We help people make better financial decisions that bring freedom and richness to their lives."
- **Core values:** Open and honest communication about risks; trust built through expertise, ongoing care, and respectful partnership; explaining complex topics in simple, human terms.
- **Brand concept:** Bringing light to the world of money/finance. The "Spark" symbol represents this core mission from the very beginning.
- **Positioning:** Modern financial advisory partner working with 50+ financial institutions. Services include mortgages, investments, insurance, and personal finance management.

## Logo
- **Structure:** Symbol (Spark) + wordmark. This is the absolute/only permitted version -- no other iterations allowed.
- **Symbol:** The "Spark" -- a star shape representing bringing light to finance. Based on a circle with diameter 7/5 the height of the wordmark, with six arms evenly spaced at 60 degrees.
- **Construction:** Built on a uniform grid determining height, stroke thicknesses, and spacing. Distance between symbol and wordmark is precisely defined.
- **Safe zone:** Defined proportionally based on the construction grid.
- **Color usage:** Specific rules for logo on coloured backgrounds to maintain contrast. No deforming, rotating, or using in inappropriate color combinations, colors outside defined palette, on distracting photographic backgrounds, or without the symbol.
- **Minimum size:** Print: 20mm, Digital: 120px.
- **Placement:** Fixed position -- always placed in the bottom left corner of the format.
- **Favicon:** Symbol in accent green, positioned centrally based on logotype construction.

## Typography
- **Primary typeface:** Macan and Macan Stencil (weights: Regular, Book, Medium, SemiBold).
- **Usage:** Macan for headings, subheadings, body text, and small text. Macan Stencil used solely to highlight parts of headings.
- **System alternative:** Arial Regular & Bold. Headline highlight styles not permitted in system alternative.
- **Hierarchy:** Built on proportional relationships. All font sizes (except Highlight) derive from base text size (Body Large = 100%). Line height ranges from 90% to 140%.
- **Headline Highlight:** Used as a subheading or emphasis that quickly summarises the topic or sets context for the main headline. Uses Macan Stencil.
- **Table typography:** Two variants -- (1) Arial Bold + Regular, (2) Macan SemiBold + Regular. Examples show financial data tables with Slovak content (bank comparisons, interest rates).

## Color Palette
- **Primary colors:**
  - Deep Green -- adds depth, calmness, and trustworthiness
  - Neon Green -- highlights what is most important
  - Clear White -- brings clarity, purity, and organisation
- **Secondary colors:** Red, Purple, and Grey, each available in 5 shades (700 darkest to 300 lightest, base = 500).
  - **Grey:** 700 (#555959A), 600 (#808587), 500 (#808587), 400 (#CED3D4), 300 (#F1F1F1)
  - **Purple:** 700 (#424C8C), 600 (#6471C2), 500 (#98A6FF), 400 (#C1CBFF), 300 (#E0E5FF)
  - **Red:** 700 (#7C2D2A), 600 (#BA543F), 500 (#F87B54), 400 (#FFAD91), 300 (#FFEDDE)
- **Color hierarchy:** Primary Deep Green and Clear White have largest representation. Neon Green used to highlight important elements that should "shine." Secondary mid shades used when more than Neon Green is needed. Lighter shades primarily for tables, graphs, and web applications. Dark shades only in extreme situations when all other options are insufficient.

## Graphic Elements
- **Core visual element:** The Spark symbol inside a neon green "LightBox" -- represents bringing light to finance.
- **LightGuide:** The symbol serves as a guide to the brand. Star positioned centrally according to logotype construction, offset = 3x (where x = 1/5 wordmark height). LightBox rounding defined by a 13x13 grid.
- **Tags:** Visual tags derived from LightGuide shape to highlight key information. Corner rounding never greater than 15.38% of total height. Used for: (1) with photographs, (2) bearing icons, (3) calls to action. Examples: "+0,45%", "Registrovať", "Pridaj sa", dates, URLs.
- **Symbol usage (LightGuide):** (1) Can bear a photograph, (2) highlight content within a photograph, (3) carry written information.
- **Iconography:** Isometric illustration-style icons that enhance information and support visual appeal. The isometric drawing adds contrast and playfulness to the brand.
- **Layout system:** Logo and LightGuide placed as far apart as format allows, positioned diagonally, content aligned according to two edges. Both vertical and horizontal layout variants defined.

## Photography
- **Style:** Neutral, natural character; grey tones should be present. Impression must be professional, reliable, and human.
- **Avoid:** Photographs that are overly bright or artificial. Images should not be overly colourful or flashy. Must convey professionalism and authenticity.

## Tone of Voice
- **Approach:** Explain as if explaining to a friend. Human, simple language for complex financial topics.
- **Values in communication:** Open, honest about risks. Trust through expertise and ongoing care. Respectful partnership approach.
- **Copy examples (Slovak):** "Rozumieme peniazom a vysvetlime ich aj vam" (We understand money and we'll explain it to you too), "Hladate svoje vysnivane byvanie?" (Looking for your dream home?), "Hladame skutocnych ludi" (We're looking for real people).
- **Implied tone:** Approachable, expert, caring, human, transparent.

## Outliers / Unique Elements
- **Macan Stencil as highlight font:** Unusual choice -- using a stencil variant of the primary typeface exclusively for headline highlights creates a distinctive visual signature.
- **LightBox/LightGuide system:** Sophisticated graphic device -- the Spark symbol inside a rounded container serves multiple functions (photo frame, content highlight, information carrier, CTA container). Well-systematized with precise construction rules.
- **Tiered business collateral:** Junior/Mid vs Senior/C-Level differentiation in business cards and folders is uncommon and shows organisational sophistication.
- **Isometric iconography:** Choosing isometric illustration-style icons for a financial services brand is distinctive -- adds playfulness and modernity to counter the serious nature of finance.
- **Financial services brand with tech-forward aesthetic:** Deep green + neon green palette with systematic shade scales (700-300) feels more like a fintech/SaaS brand than traditional financial advisory.
- **Diagonal layout principle:** Logo and LightGuide positioned diagonally at maximum distance is a strong, distinctive compositional rule.
