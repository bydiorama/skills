## Logo Guidelines

Thorough coverage across pages 3--18, structured as follows:

### Primary logo (Primarne logo)
- Composed of three elements: **symbol** (geometric mark built from trapezoids), **acronym** ("DEUS"), and **full name** of the association displayed in four lines (one line per letter of the acronym).
- All elements are typeset in **Fedra Sans Alt Pro** with no custom modifications.
  - Acronym + symbol: **Fedra Sans Alt Pro Medium**
  - Full name text: **Fedra Sans Alt Pro Book**

### Color variants
Three equal-weight color variants for the logo:
1. **Black** (Pantone Black 6 C)
2. **Gold** (Pantone 4645 C)
3. **Grey** (Pantone Cool Gray 6 C)

A **white** version is permitted only when none of the primary variants can provide sufficient contrast or due to production/print limitations.

### Geometry and clear space
- Logo geometry is derived from the letterforms of Fedra Sans Alt Pro.
- Spacing between symbol, acronym, and text is defined as **1.5x the width of one trapezoid** from the symbol.
- **Clear space (optimal):** 2x the trapezoid unit on all sides.
- **Clear space (minimum):** 1x the longer dimension of the trapezoid.

### Shortened logo
- Symbol + acronym without the full name. Preferred when full name would be too small to read or when the association is already contextually identifiable.
- Same three color variants and clear-space rules apply.

### Symbol standalone
- The symbol alone is **not** considered a full substitute for the logo.
- Acceptable as a supplementary element when the primary or shortened logo is already present (e.g., multi-page brochures, business card reverse, favicon).

### Prohibited uses (page 18)
Six explicit prohibitions illustrated:
1. Low-contrast background/logo combinations
2. Rotation
3. Non-proportional scaling
4. Incorrect colorway
5. Placement inside arbitrary geometric shapes
6. Placement on photographs with insufficient contrast

## Typography

Covered on pages 19--21.

### Primary typeface
- **Fedra Sans Alt Pro** by Peter Bilak (Slovak type designer)
- Used weights:
  - **Fedra Sans Alt Pro Medium** -- headings and subheadings
  - **Fedra Sans Alt Pro Book** -- body text and longer copy
- Full glyph set shown including Central European diacritical characters.

### Alternative / fallback typeface
- **Arial** (system font, Windows and macOS)
  - **Arial Bold** -- headings
  - **Arial Regular** -- body
- Reserved primarily for internal and electronic communication (email, press releases).
- The manual explicitly notes that the authors do not consider Arial a craftsmanship-equal replacement for the Fedra Sans family.

## Supporting Graphics

Covered on pages 24--28 under "Vzor" (Pattern).

- Graphic patterns are derived from the **symbol's outline** (trapezoid shape).
- Permitted pattern operations: multiplication, repetition, rotation, and scaling of the symbol outline.
- Patterns may use all colors from the palette, including **color gradients** between palette colors.
- Three pattern examples are shown:
  1. Multiplication + rotation + scaling (abstract, layered composition)
  2. Multiplication + rotation + scaling + color gradient
  3. Simple repetition (tiled grid of the symbol outline)

## Outliers / Notable

1. **Slovak designer heritage:** The choice of Fedra Sans Alt Pro by Peter Bilak (a prominent Slovak type designer) deliberately ties the visual identity to Slovak design culture -- a meaningful detail for a Slovak association.
2. **Ethical sourcing for merch:** The manual explicitly recommends FairTrade or FairWear-certified textile suppliers, reflecting the association's values (ethics, sustainability) even at the brand-guidelines level.
3. **Candid note on Arial:** The authors openly state they do not consider Arial a craftsmanship-equal substitute for Fedra Sans -- an unusually honest remark in a brand manual.
4. **No brand strategy layer:** For an association whose name literally spells out ethics, sustainability, and society, the absence of any strategic/verbal brand framework is a notable gap.
5. **Embossing and lamination guidance:** Specific print-finishing recommendations (kashovanie/razenie) for business cards suggest a premium physical identity despite the non-profit context.

## Quality Score

**3.5 / 5**

**Strengths:**
- Comprehensive logo system with clear hierarchy (primary, shortened, symbol-only) and well-defined geometry and clear-space rules.
- Full color specifications across Pantone, CMYK, RGB, and HEX.
- Thoughtful pattern system derived from the logo symbol.
- Good range of application examples.
- Professional layout and production (Adobe InDesign, 39 pages).

**Weaknesses:**
- No brand strategy, positioning, or values section.
- No tone of voice or verbal identity.
- Photography direction is minimal (two sentences in a social media context).
- Typography section lacks sizing, leading, or hierarchy specifications -- only weights are defined.
- No digital/web-specific guidelines (responsive behavior, UI components, accessibility).
- Color tints (75%, 50%, 25%) are shown visually but their exact CMYK/RGB/HEX values are not provided.
