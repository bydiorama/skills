## Typography
- **Primary typeface: Labil Grotesk Bold**
- Described as "the voice of the festival -- expressive, grounded, and unmistakably offbeat"
- Skewed structure and exaggerated weight embody the spirit of jazz: "unpolished yet refined, structured yet free"
- Used for artist names, headlines, and moments where typographic personality takes the lead
- Distinctive character forms: mirrored "J", slightly irregular letterforms with organic curves
- Full character set shown including diacritics (important for Slovak text)
- The font's irregularity/imperfection is a deliberate design choice reflecting jazz improvisation
