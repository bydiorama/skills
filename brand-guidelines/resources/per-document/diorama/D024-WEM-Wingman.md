## Brand Strategy
- Sub-brand of WEM (parent company in finance/investment); tagline "powered by WEM"
- Document structure: Logotype (p3), Colorway (p10), Typography (p13), Visual Guidelines (p16), Applications (p21)
- Dark, sophisticated cover aesthetic -- dark brown/charcoal background with cream/off-white type and pink-toned wing icon

## Logo
- Logomark: Stylized wing icon made of three layered, swept-back wing shapes with a gradient from deeper rose/red at the bottom to lighter pink at the top
- Logotype: "Wingman" in a bold, geometric sans-serif with distinctive rounded letterforms (especially the "g" and "a")
- Lockup includes "powered by WEM" in smaller white caps below the wordmark
- Logo shown on dark background (primary presentation)
- Safety zone: defined by the width of the letter "n" on all sides of the logotype
- Four approved color variations shown in a 2x2 grid:
  - Light background + full-color wing (rose/pink gradient) with dark wordmark
  - Dark background + full-color wing (rose/pink gradient) with light/cream wordmark
  - Light background + monochrome dark wing with dark wordmark
  - Dark background + monochrome light wing with light/cream wordmark
- Wing icon always appears to the left of the wordmark in horizontal lockup

## Typography
- Section starts on page 13 (divider page with owl wing imagery)
- Logotype uses a bold geometric sans-serif with distinctive rounded letterforms
- Body text appears to use a clean sans-serif (visible in email signature application)
- Hierarchy visible in applications: bold italic headlines, regular weight body copy

## Color Palette
- Three-column color system with full tint/shade scales (100-900 for each):
  - **Neutral/Warm Beige:** Range from near-white (100) to warm taupe/brown (900); primary color at 500 level (warm sand/beige)
  - **Rose/Red:** Range from pale pink (100) to near-black dark red (900); primary color at 500 level (dusty rose, muted terracotta-red)
  - **Neutral/Cool Gray:** Range from very light gray (100) to near-black charcoal (900); primary color at 900 level (dark charcoal-brown, used as the dominant dark tone)
- Palette is muted, warm, and earthy -- avoiding bright or saturated hues
- The rose color is the signature accent, connecting to the wing logomark gradient

## Graphics / Visual Language
- "Symbol Crop" technique: the wing logomark is blown up to extreme scale and cropped, used as a full-bleed background pattern
- Rendered in tonal beige/neutral shades (monochrome, no color accent) creating subtle, textural brand surfaces
- The layered wing stripes become an abstract geometric pattern when enlarged -- works as a sophisticated background device

## Photography
- "Owl Images -- Crops" page shows close-up, desaturated photographs of owl wings
- Images are tightly cropped, showing feather texture and wing structure in detail
- Warm, muted, nearly monochrome toning -- beige/sepia aesthetic matching the brand palette
- Owl wing imagery reinforces the "Wingman" name and wing logomark concept
- Photography style: nature/macro, editorial quality, understated and elegant

## Tone of Voice
- Headlines are short, declarative, and confident:
  - "ILLUMINATES DATA. LEAVES DECISIONS TO YOU."
  - "WHEN OTHERS GUESS, WE SEE."
  - "CLARITY IS A COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE."
  - "DESIGNED TO REVEAL WHAT COUNTS."
- Positioning: Wingman as a clear-sighted, data-driven investment platform that empowers user autonomy
- Tone is authoritative yet approachable; emphasizes clarity, insight, and professional individualized service
- Body copy (Slovak): emphasizes ETF investing, self-directed investing, professional and individualized approach

## Outliers / Unique Elements
- Owl as the spirit animal / brand mascot -- reinforces "Wingman" name through wing imagery and owl's association with wisdom and sharp vision (fitting for an investment platform)
- Duotone red effect applied to photography in some applications (hand holding phone)
- The "powered by WEM" sub-brand architecture links this product brand to its parent financial company
- Slovak-market focus (wingman.sk, Slovak-language copy)
- Symbol crop technique used as abstract geometric backgrounds is a distinctive design device
