## Outliers / Notable

1. **Dual-language approach:** The document structure and labels are in English, but application copy is predominantly in Slovak. This reflects a Slovak-market brand with internationally structured guidelines.

2. **Emblem as layout device:** The emblem system with three placement modes (next to headline, enclosing layout, accompanying copy) is a sophisticated graphic device that goes beyond typical logo usage rules. It functions as a compositional tool.

3. **Photography color control:** The inclusion of both a recommended color palette and an "avoid" palette specifically for photography is a strong production detail. This ensures photographic assets maintain tonal consistency with the brand palette.

4. **Color ratio specification:** The explicit percentage-based color ratio (50/20/10/16/5) provides precise guidance for maintaining visual balance. The 16% allocation including text is an unusually specific detail.

5. **Financial compliance details:** The letterhead includes company registration numbers (ICO, DIC, IC DPH) -- reflecting the regulated financial services context where legal compliance extends into brand materials.

6. **Video branding:** Including lower-third specifications and end-screen templates shows forward-thinking for a 2021 financial services brand book.

7. **Sofia Pro + Georgia pairing:** The geometric sans + humanist serif pairing is well-considered -- Sofia Pro brings modernity while Georgia brings established credibility. This is particularly appropriate for wealth management.

8. **Related document:** A separate WEM Private Fund brand guidelines document exists in this collection (D012), suggesting a sub-brand architecture.

**Overall assessment:** A well-structured design guidelines document for a premium financial services brand.
