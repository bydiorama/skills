## Brand Strategy

This is a comprehensive brand manual that opens with a full strategy section (pp 2-9), written in Slovak.

### Values (Hodnoty)
1. **Tradicia (Tradition)** -- Vratna is a place of history and tradition. They preserve and develop Mala Fatra with respect for its genius loci. Reliability that keeps people coming back. Creating a legacy for future generations.
2. **Komfort (Comfort)** -- Everything is done to make visitors feel their best. Comfort is ensured everywhere: accessibility of ridges via cable cars, friendly communications, service offerings, quality navigation, and regional relationship building.
3. **Symbioza (Symbiosis)** -- Delivering experiences for people with respect and care for nature. Building long-term relationships with people, nature, and regional businesses. A place that values sustainability and satisfaction of all.

### Belief (Presvedcenie)
Active time spent in nature brings peace, makes people happier and better. Positively affects mental health, body, and interpersonal relationships. Vratna commits to creating an environment where a harmonious relationship between people and nature is within arm's reach.

### Purpose (Zmysel)
"Pomahame ludom vytvorit si autenticky vztah k prirode, vdaka ktoremu dokazu zit naplneny zivot." -- Helping people create an authentic relationship with nature, enabling them to live a fulfilled life.

### Vision (Vizia)
To become a home for everyone seeking refuge and inspiration in nature. Striving to create an environment where everyone can experience the true essence of rest and experiences in harmony with nature.

### Mission (Misia)
"Vytvarame nezabudnutelne zazitky pre ludi s respektom k prirode." -- Creating unforgettable experiences for people with respect for nature.

## Logo Guidelines

### Primary Logotype (pp 16-17)
- Clean wordmark "Vratna" -- simple, bold, distinctive, modern and highly legible.
- Described as the cornerstone of the brand. Simple yet expressive, enabling easy identification and memorability.

### Variants
1. **Basic version** (p 17) -- Standard dark-on-light logotype.
2. **Invert** (p 18) -- White on dark / colored backgrounds.
3. **On photography** (p 19) -- Logo placed over photographic imagery, white version with adequate contrast.
4. **Mala Fatra sub-line** (p 20) -- Extended version adding the regional name "Mala Fatra" beneath the wordmark, reinforcing geographic identity and connection to the Mala Fatra mountain range.

### Misuse Rules (p 21)
- **No deformation** -- Logo must never be stretched, compressed, or distorted. Original proportions and integrity must be maintained.
- **No color changes** -- The wordmark must always be presented in brand colors. Non-standard colors may disrupt the identity.
- No additional graphic elements or decorative effects that could impair legibility or recognizability.

### Clear Space
- Not explicitly documented with measurement units in the extracted text, though visual examples likely demonstrate spacing rules.

## Typography

### Primary / Headline Typeface (p 30)
- **Sans Plomb 98** by **Lift Type** (lift-type.fr)
- Modern, clean, highly legible typeface reflecting professionalism and approachable character.
- Used for all major text elements including headlines and the logo.
- Clean lines and open forms ensure excellent readability even at smaller sizes across various media.
- Source: https://www.lift-type.fr/shop/typography/sans-plomb/

### Secondary / Body Typeface (p 31)
- **Haffer** (Regular + Bold) by **Displaay Type Foundry** (displaay.net)
- Complements Sans Plomb. Its characteristic features add depth and texture to the typography.
- Used for body text, subheadlines, and supplementary text elements. Unique character and readability make it ideal for longer passages and informational sections.
- Source: https://displaay.net/typeface/haffer-collection/haffer/

### Websafe Fallback (p 33)
- **Arial** (Regular + Bold) -- permitted alternative when Haffer is unavailable.
- Sans Plomb 98 remains preferred for headlines even when the fallback is in use.
- Haffer and Arial are not to be combined -- only one body family may be used at a time.

### Typographic Hierarchy (pp 32, 34)
| Level | Size | Line Height |

| Subheadline | 100% | 1.0 |
| Copy Large | 120% | 1.0 |

## Tone of Voice

### Communication Style (pp 12-13)
- **Human, empathetic, approachable, trustworthy, caring, and efficient.**
- Goal: Make people's time in nature as pleasant as possible, relieving them from everyday worries.
- **Language:** Clear, simple, and understandable, accessible to everyone regardless of their previous experience with outdoor activities.
- **Avoid:** Complex technical/expert language. Always explain terms that may be new to someone.
- **Emotional register:** Communicate with heart and soul, ensuring messages resonate on an emotional level.

### Claim System (pp 10-11)
- Core claim "Hor sa." references tradition and mountains. Sounds welcoming and colloquial, while also being a call to action.
- Modular claim structure enables contextual messaging:
  - Hor sa do prirody. (to nature)
  - Hor sa na lyze. (to ski)
  - Hor sa na turu. (to hike)
  - Hor sa za zazitkam. (for experiences)
  - Hor sa za dobrodruzstvom. (for adventure)
  - Hor sa do Vratnej. (to Vratna)

## Outliers / Notable

1. **Exceptional comprehensiveness** -- At 101 pages, this is one of the most complete brand guidelines in the collection. It covers brand strategy, visual identity, and an unusually wide range of real-world applications including restaurant menus, ski passes, vehicle wraps, navigation systems, and merchandise.
2. **Deep strategy foundation** -- Unlike many resort/hospitality brand guides that jump straight to visual identity, this document opens with a full strategic framework (values, belief, purpose, vision, mission) before any visual elements.
3. **Modular claim system** -- The "Hor sa" claim with its fill-in-the-blank modularity is a sophisticated branding device that enables flexible yet consistent messaging across all touchpoints.
4. **40 custom pictograms** -- An extensive custom icon system tailored specifically to resort operations (ratrak, snow cannon, ski bus, mountain tricycle, HZS) goes well beyond generic icon sets.
5. **CMYK data error** -- Cold Green's CMYK values (98, 194, 177) exceed the valid 0-100 range, suggesting a copy-paste error from RGB values.
6. **Bilingual signage** -- Navigation and warning signs include English translations alongside Slovak, indicating international visitor awareness.
7. **Real-world specificity** -- Application examples use real data (actual menu items with prices, real staff names, actual vehicle models in fleet), making this a working production document rather than a conceptual exercise.
8. **Premium typeface selection** -- Both Sans Plomb 98 (Lift Type) and Haffer (Displaay) are boutique foundry typefaces, signaling investment in typographic distinction.
9. **Color palette inspired by local flora** -- Brand Violet is specifically tied to the "poniklec" (pasque flower), grounding the color system in the local natural environment.
10. **Operating entity** -- The resort is operated by OMNITRADE a.s. based in Bratislava, with the resort located in Terchova (Stary Dvor, Paseky), 013 06 postal code.
