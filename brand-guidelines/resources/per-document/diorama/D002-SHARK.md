## Brand Strategy
- **Mission/Vision/Purpose**: The brand identity is described as more than just a logo — it is a system of elements that reinforce core brand values: courage ("odvaha"), enthusiasm ("nadsenie"), and customer focus ("sustredenie sa na zakaznika").
- **Values**: Courage, Enthusiasm, Customer Focus
- **Positioning**: Technology retailer (SHARK Computers, a. s.) positioned as an experience-driven electronics brand, emphasis on the enjoyment and utility of technology rather than just product specs.
- **Target Audience**: Not explicitly segmented, but B2B is mentioned for business cards; B2C consumer electronics shoppers implied throughout. Slovak market (Bratislava HQ).
- **Brand Personality/Archetype**: Bold, geometric, modern. The visual system leans toward a confident, clean, tech-forward personality — sharp edges, strong contrast, geometric forms.

## Logo Guidelines
- **Logo type**: Wordmark based on the typeface **Termina** (by Mattox Shuler), set in Regular weight.
- **Symbol**: The letter "A" in SHARK has its crossbar removed, forming a standalone lambda/triangle symbol. This symbol can be used independently when the full wordmark cannot be displayed legibly.
- **Clear space**: Defined by the height of the letter "H" in the wordmark. The logo is ideally placed within a square whose edge length derives from the clear space measurement.
- **Minimum size**: When displayed at small sizes, the Regular weight may be swapped for **Medium** weight to maintain legibility. Minimum clear space must still be observed.
- **Color variations**:
  - Negative space (white logo on dark background) — primary/preferred usage
  - Positive space (dark logo on light background)
  - Color background versions (using brand turquoise or gradients)
  - Symbol also available in filled color/gradient versions in positive display
- **Incorrect usage examples** (page 12): Deformation (stretching), low contrast between logo and background, incorrect colors, rotation/tilting, and displaying the symbol alongside the full logo simultaneously (duplicating the "A"/lambda).
- **Construction grid**: The logo derives directly from the Termina typeface geometry; the symbol is derived by removing the crossbar from "A".

## Typography
- **Primary typeface**: **Gilroy** (by Radomir Tinkov) — geometric sans-serif
  - Recommended weights: Black, Bold, Medium, Regular, Light
  - Headlines/subheadlines use bold/black weights; body text uses Regular and Medium
  - Full Central European character set
- **Secondary typeface (alternative)**: **Montserrat** (by Julieta Ulanovsky) — free under SIL OFL license
  - Used when Gilroy is unavailable for legal or technical reasons
  - Same weight range: Black, Bold, Medium, Regular, Light
  - Primary substitute for web and office use
- **System fallback**: **Verdana** — used in email signatures and contexts where custom fonts are unsupported
- **Logo typeface**: **Termina** (by Mattox Shuler) — used exclusively for the wordmark and symbol derivation, Regular and Medium weights
- **Hierarchy levels** (page 28):
  - SEQUENCER: Black, 900%, line-height 1.10
  - HEADLINE/TITLE: Black, 500%, line-height 1.10
  - SECTION: Black, 300%, line-height 1.25
  - COPYTEXT HEADLINE: Medium, 200%, line-height 1.25
  - COPY: Regular, 100%, line-height 1.5
  - CAPTION HEADLINE: Regular, 80%, line-height 1.5
  - CAPTION: Regular, 80%, line-height 1.5
- **Web vs print**: Gilroy for print/primary, Montserrat for web/office, Verdana as system fallback. Email newsletters use 15px base, 18-20px headings, 30px element spacing.
- **Type scale**: Percentage-based relative scale from 80% (caption) to 900% (sequencer/display)

## Supporting Graphics
- **Square grid system**: The square is the fundamental building block of the visual identity. Surfaces are divided progressively into 4 and then 16 parts to create a mosaic texture pattern.
- **Mosaic texture**: Created by subdividing square surfaces with color tints. Can be perforated by omitting certain squares (replacing with white). Applied in print (die-cutting), interior design, and product installations.
- **Color mosaic rules**: Mosaics should combine tints of a single color systematically; multiple colors can be used but should follow the tint-combination system shown in the guidelines. The grid should not become too dense/fine.
- **Gradients**: Two-color gradients preferred, using colors of similar hue:
  - Gradient 1: Turquoise to Deep Ocean
  - Gradient 2: Pearl Aqua to Deep Ocean
  - Gradient 3: White to Pearl Aqua
  - Gradient 4: Orange-red to Banana Yellow
- **Lambda symbol variants**: The base lambda symbol can be rotated, flipped, and modified to create a series of graphic navigation/communication elements (icons for online/offline wayfinding).
- **Icon system**: Derived from the lambda symbol through geometric transformations (page 30)
- **Patterns**: Square mosaic subdivision pattern, applicable across media

## Photography
- **Style guidelines**: Content focuses on people and emotions over pure product shots. The lookbook prioritizes the enjoyment and utility of technology ("potesenie a uzitok z technologii").
- **Art direction**: Full-color imagery combined with brand-colored duotone/duplex treatments using SHARK brand colors.
- **Treatments**: Color duplex using brand palette; images toned in brand colors for social media.
- **Do/Don't**: Social media posts should focus on the experience of technology rather than isolated products. Product posts should integrate the brand color palette and visual identity elements.

## Application Examples
- **Business cards** (page 32): 85 x 55 mm, dark back (logo only) and light back variants. Front contains name, position, and contact info in a single line. Text offset 10mm from top and left edges. Recommended: heavy uncoated paper, optionally laminated with dark edge.
- **Lookbook/Magazine** (pages 33-37): 12-column grid layout. Text should not exceed 40% of available spread area. Max 70 characters per line. Text, logo, and URL placement varies by format.
- **Shopping bags** (pages 38-39): Square paper bags for premium products; plastic bags (transparent or turquoise) for standard purchases. Color can be varied.
- **Presentation slides** (page 40): 16:9 or 4:3 aspect ratios. Light background with dark text for office/small screens; inverted (dark background) for projectors and large screens.
- **Email signature** (page 41): Name, position, logo with contact details. Font color #333333. Verdana as system substitute.
- **Newsletter/Email** (page 42): Two types — "Announcement" email (single-column, hero visual + CTA) and "Product" email (two-column product grid with images, descriptions, and CTA buttons). Base text 15px, headings 18-20px, 30px element spacing.
- **Social media** (pages 43-45): Profile picture uses full SHARK wordmark when enlarged; lambda symbol when space is constrained. Cover images: text left-aligned top, logo top-right. Post types: experience/lifestyle image posts, product posts with brand palette integration, link posts with brand-styled preview images.
- **Digital**: Product display layouts combining typography, mosaic, and subtle gradients (page 23)
- **Environmental**: Mosaic patterns applicable to interior design and product installations via die-cutting, embossing, or spatial arrangement
- **Merchandise**: Not explicitly covered beyond bags

## Outliers / Notable Approaches
- **Lambda symbol derivation**: The approach of removing the crossbar from the letter "A" in the wordmark to create a standalone symbol (lambda) is elegant — it creates a recognizable mark that is organically tied to the wordmark rather than being a separate design.
- **Square as fundamental element**: The entire visual system is built on the square as a geometric primitive — from logo placement (always in a square container) to the mosaic texture system (progressive subdivision of squares) to the paper bag shape. This creates strong geometric consistency.
- **Weight-responsive logo**: The guideline to switch from Termina Regular to Termina Medium at small sizes is a thoughtful detail that ensures legibility without creating a separate "small" logo variant.
- **Tint system**: The systematic 100/75/50/25/15/5% tint scale for both primary and secondary colors creates a very large but controlled palette from just 5 base colors.
- **Duplex photography treatment**: Using brand colors for photographic duotone treatments is a strong unifying technique for lifestyle imagery.
- **Practical email specifications**: Pixel-level specifications for email layouts (15px body, 18-20px headings, 30px spacing) show attention to real-world implementation.
