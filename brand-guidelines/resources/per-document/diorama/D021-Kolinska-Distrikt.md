## Brand Strategy

- **Brand story**: Kolinska is a former food/beverage production district (coffee, chocolate, wafers) in Ljubljana, now being redeveloped into a mixed-use urban neighborhood. The name is retained intentionally -- it carries nostalgic associations with taste and childhood flavors.
- **Claim**: "Living with full flavor"
- **Positioning**: Heritage-rooted urban regeneration; the flavor metaphor bridges the industrial food past with vibrant contemporary living.
- **Document structure** (67 pages): 1.0 Fundamentals (p3), 2.0 Logotype (p14), 3.0 Brand Typography (p31), 4.0 Brand Colors (p39), 5.0 Visual System (p45), 6.0 Applications (p54).

## Logo

- **Primary logo**: "Kolinska" wordmark in a heavy, condensed serif/slab typeface, with "DISTRIKT" set below in spaced-out sans-serif caps. Above the wordmark sits a distinctive **arch/bridge icon** -- a stylized architectural silhouette.
- **Color on cover**: Dark forest green logotype on light sage/pistachio green background.
- **Shape language**: The cover background features an organic, angular cutout shape (like a stylized building silhouette or crystalline form) -- this may be a recurring brand shape device.
- **Gable icon**: The arch element above the wordmark is a graphic "gable" extracted from the original factory facade architecture. Designed for flexibility -- enables future brand family extensions.
- **Wordmark construction**: "Kolinska" with only first letter capitalized. Heavy condensed serif letterforms in dark forest green. The "K" has distinctive geometric cuts.
- **Logo system depth**: 13 sub-sections covering default logotype, construction grids, safe zones, tagline variant, permitted colors, color versions, forbidden use, minimum size, favicon, placement in graphics, and developer co-branding (Corwin).
- **Logo on light background**: Dark green wordmark + sage/muted green gable icon on warm cream (#F5EFDF approx.).

## Typography

- **Primary typeface**: Field Gothic XBold Condensed -- used only for headings and short texts inside label shapes. Fallback: Arial Narrow.
- **Secondary typeface**: Azeret (Regular + Semibold) -- used for body text and titles within longer content. Fallback: Arial Regular / Bold.
- **Type scale** (px-based, designed at 1920x1080):
  - Headline: 260px / 90% line-height / -4% tracking
  - Subheadline: 130px / 90% line-height / -3% tracking
  - Copy Large: 72px / 140% line-height / -2% tracking
  - Copy Medium: 60px / 150% line-height / -2% tracking
  - Copy Small: 48px / 150% line-height / 0% tracking
- **Layout rules**: Left or center alignment permitted. Gap between headline and body must be at least 2x the headline line-height.
- **Combined headlines**: Headlines can integrate symbols from the brand set (max 2-3 symbols per headline). Symbol size matches caps height; spacing equals line height.
- **Arch type claim**: Curved/arched text element used on key visuals, social media, and print. Arc follows an 80% height circle, centered. Always set in Azeret Regular. Always reads "Living with full flavor".

## Color Palette

- **Naming convention**: Colors are encoded as food names -- a tribute to the former food factory.
- **Dark tones** (5 primaries):
  - Cola: #1C1917 (near-black warm brown) / Pantone Black 6 C
  - Avocado: #395C3E (forest green) / Pantone 7735 C
  - Blueberry: #3B4952 (dark blue-grey) / Pantone 2379 C
  - Cookie: #7E461A (warm brown/amber) / Pantone 725 C
  - Wine: #9B1010 (deep red) / Pantone 2350 C
- **Light tints** (5 paired tints):
  - Cream: #FFF7E9 (warm off-white) / Pantone 7401 C 30%
  - Sugar: #DBEACD (pale sage green) / Pantone 2254 C 50%
  - Frosting: #E3EFF5 (ice blue) / Pantone 656 C
  - Oat: #FFEBC2 (warm peach/buff) / Pantone 7507 C 70%
  - Bubblegum: #FFD9CC (soft pink) / Pantone 4033 C
- **Usage rule**: Max 2-3 colors per layout; 2 must come from a tint pair (dark + light). White and black used only for text legibility and logotype.
- **Shade system**: Each dark tone has a 6-step opacity scale (100%, 80%, 60%, 40%, 20%, 10%), primarily used for the gable symbol coloring.

## Graphic Elements

- **The Label** (core visual device): Inspired by food packaging/label design from the factory heritage. The label shape functions as a foundational framing element across the identity.
- **Label library**: 10 unique label shapes derived from architectural landmarks and visual study of the site. Used as standalone elements, cropped background frames, or sticker/CTA elements.
- **Background/frame crop**: Label shapes can be cropped to create background frames and compositional structures.
- **Sticker / Call to action**: Label shapes serve as containers for CTA content.
- **Quotation marks**: Large decorative quotation marks with mosaic/checkered fill pattern used on claim pages.

## Tone of Voice

- **Naming rationale**: Name "Kolinska" deliberately retained from the former food factory -- carries childhood nostalgia and taste associations. No renaming needed; the legacy is the asset.
- **Claim**: "Living with full flavor" -- uses sensory/taste metaphor to connect heritage (food production) with contemporary urban living.
- **Voice character** (from copy samples): Warm, evocative, sensory-rich. Short declarative sentences mixed with poetic imagery. Second-person address ("it's yours to discover"). Conversational but elevated. Emphasizes moments, experiences, and everyday rituals (espresso, market stalls, wine).
- **Key line**: "Kolinska isn't just a place to live -- it's a place to taste life."

## Outliers / Unique Elements

- **Food-coded color naming**: Every color in the palette is named after a food/drink (Cola, Avocado, Blueberry, Cookie, Wine, Cream, Sugar, Frosting, Oat, Bubblegum) -- deeply reinforces the flavor narrative at every touchpoint.
- **Label as structural device**: The packaging-label metaphor is unusually well-developed -- 10 unique shapes forming a full library, each usable in 3 modes (standalone, cropped frame, sticker/CTA). This goes well beyond typical shape libraries.
- **Arch type claim**: Curved text on an 80%-height circle is a distinctive and precisely specified typographic device -- uncommon in real estate branding.
- **Gable icon as brand family seed**: Explicitly designed to allow future sub-brand extensions under the Kolinska umbrella.
- **Five permitted logo color modes**: Two-tone positive, monotone positive, two-tone negative (on dark brown), monotone negative (white on dark brown), and monotone black. The two-tone variants use the muted sage gable with dark green wordmark.
- **Sensory brand strategy for real estate**: The entire brand is built on taste/flavor metaphors for a property development -- an unusual and cohesive strategic choice.
