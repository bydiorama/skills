## Logo Guidelines

### Logotype (Primary)
- The logotype consists of a **symbol** (lock/eye icon) paired with the **wordmark** "ssleek" set in **Gellix Medium**.
- The name is styled entirely in lowercase: **ssleek** (double "s").
- **Primary version:** Purple (#675EAB) logo on white background (p.4).
- **Inverse version:** White logo on purple (#675EAB) background or on photography/colored backgrounds, provided sufficient contrast is maintained (p.5).

### Clear Space
- Full logotype clear space is defined by the **width and height of the letter "s"** applied equally on all four sides (p.6).
- Symbol-only clear space is defined by the **diameter of the "pupil" / "keyhole"** element applied on all four sides (p.8).

### Symbol (Standalone)
- The symbol can be described as a **lock/eye** hybrid -- a circle containing a horizontal bar with a half-circle (pupil/keyhole) element.
- Permitted for standalone use without the wordmark in internal communications or small-format applications (p.7).
- Available in both purple-on-white and white-on-purple versions.

### Logo Variants Observed
1. Full logo (symbol + wordmark) -- primary
2. Full logo inverse (white on purple)
3. Symbol only (lock/eye)
4. Wordmark only ("ssleek" text) -- seen on stickers (p.15)

### Misuse Rules
- Not explicitly documented in this version of the guidelines.

## Outliers / Notable

1. **Double "s" in name:** The brand name is "ssleek" with a double "s" -- an intentional stylistic choice. This is consistent across all applications.
2. **Symbol dual meaning:** The symbol explicitly represents both a **lock** (security) and an **eye** (monitoring/visibility) -- a clever conceptual pairing for a certificate management product.
3. **Extremely minimal palette:** Only three colors (purple, white, black) with no secondary or accent colors. This is unusually restrained even for a tech brand.
4. **Single typeface, single weight specified:** Only Gellix Medium is formally named, though a heavier weight appears in the specimen. No secondary or web-safe fallback fonts are provided.
5. **No misuse rules:** The guidelines lack a "don'ts" section showing prohibited logo treatments, which is unusual for a brand manual.
6. **No digital/UI specifications:** No favicon sizes, app icon specs, social media avatar guidelines, or responsive sizing rules -- notable given this is a SaaS product.
7. **Slovak language throughout:** All descriptive text is in Slovak, suggesting the agency (Bonet) and primary audience are Slovak/Czech.
8. **High-quality mockups:** The application examples use photorealistic 3D mockups (stone surfaces, nighttime citylight scene), indicating strong production values despite the document's brevity.
9. **Presentation format:** The 1920x1080 landscape page size suggests this was created as a presentation deck rather than a traditional print-oriented brand book.
10. **RAL codes included:** The inclusion of RAL color codes alongside Pantone suggests consideration for physical signage, architectural elements, or product hardware applications.
