## Brand Strategy

- **Tagline / Motto:** "Nothing Venture, Nothing Win." (Edmund Hillary quote)
- **Positioning:** George Hillary -- grandson of Sir Edmund Hillary -- personal brand as explorer, storyteller, speaker
- **Purpose:** "Inspire, Uplift, and Connect" -- democratize mountaineering stories and inspire a new generation to care about mountains and exploration
- **Mission:** Continue Edmund Hillary's mission of making mountains accessible to all, with modern transparency and authenticity
- **Unique Value:** "Honest storytelling that makes mountains and the Hillary legacy accessible to all"
- **Core Pillars:** The Legacy Connection (standing on shoulders of giants with humility, respect, and a smile) + Additional Layers (psychology of performance, honest challenges, cross-generational connection, practical climbing advice)
- **Values:** Authenticity, Balance, Connection, Innovation
- **Brand Archetypes (triple archetype model):**
  - Explorer-Seeker (curious and adventurous) -- primary
  - Citizen-Networker (community-oriented, connecting people)
  - Guardian-Caregiver (supportive and protective)
- **Personality:** Conversational, Honest, Warm, Authentic, Focused, Humorous
- **Immediate objectives:** Build commercial viability for speaking career; philanthropic impact through visibility; transition from FinTech to full-time exploration/speaking
- **Long-term objectives:** First-name recognition ("George" not "Hillary's grandson"); mountain preservation advocacy; become a "David Attenborough figure for mountaineering"
- **Audience tiers (altitude metaphor):**
  - Sea Level -- universal human stories anyone can relate to
  - Base Camp -- adventure stories with life lessons, celebrating heroes and history
  - Summit -- deep dives, technical insights in accessible narrative
- **Content principle:** Every piece should resonate with at least two audience layers; if both a corporate executive and a climbing guide find value, it works

## Logo

- **Wordmark-based identity:** "George" in bold rounded sans-serif + "Hillary" in a slab/display serif with horizontal line details through letterforms
- **Cover layout:** "George" large top-left, "Hillary" rotated vertically along right edge -- creates an asymmetric, modern composition
- **No standalone icon/symbol observed** -- the name itself is the brand mark
- **Footer treatment:** "George Hillary" set in a smaller, lighter weight for attribution

## Typography

- **Primary display:** Bold, rounded sans-serif (used for "George") -- appears to be a geometric sans in the vein of Nunito Bold or similar rounded grotesque
- **Secondary display:** Slab-style treatment for "Hillary" with distinctive horizontal strikethrough details in letterforms (H, i, l characters have embedded horizontal bars)
- **Body text:** Clean sans-serif, medium weight, used for paragraph content throughout the guidelines
- **Quote/attribution text:** Italicized serif or semi-serif for Edmund Hillary attribution

## Tone of Voice

- **Voice characteristics:** Conversational, Honest, Warm, Authentic, Focused, Humorous
- **Content style:** Story-first approach, Visual, Contextual, Behind-the-scenes insights, Balanced perspectives
- **Tagline for TOV:** "Complex topics, simply put"
- **Opening moves:** Share personal experience/anecdote; hook with unexpected angles; never start with credentials
- **Engagement building:** Show historical context, celebrate others' achievements, describe modern reality (surprising/controversial), use universal truths
- **Do NOT use:** Overused metaphors ("journey"), obsolete terms ("conquered summit"), excessive adventure jargon without context, corporate inspiration speak
- **Key principles:** Lead with story, layer in history and expertise, maintain humor, stay humble
- **What George does NOT do:** Only present one side, ignore challenges, focus solely on own accomplishments, use jargon without explanation

## Outliers / Unique Details

- **Altitude-based content framework** (Sea Level / Base Camp / Summit) is a distinctive, brand-native organizational system rarely seen in personal brand guidelines
- **Anti-credentials approach:** Explicitly states "never start with credentials" -- unusual for a personal brand built on legacy
- **Metric: identity shift** -- tracking the shift from "Edmund's grandson" to "George the explorer who happens to be a Hillary" as a success metric
- **Revenue streams defined:** Public speaking, content courses/masterclasses, partnerships/sponsorships, boutique guided tours
- **6-month foundation / 7-12 month momentum** timeline baked into brand strategy
- **Round 02 v01** -- this is a second-round revision, indicating iterative development
- **Humor as strategic asset:** "I inherited my grandfather's humor, along with his name" -- humor is core to brand, not an afterthought
- **Triple archetype model** with visual matrix diagram -- unusually sophisticated personality mapping for a personal brand
- **Career transition documented:** Currently in FinTech, planning transition to full-time exploration/speaking -- brand guidelines as career pivot tool
- **David Attenborough aspiration:** Long-term goal explicitly stated as becoming the "David Attenborough figure for mountaineering"
- **No visual identity system:** This document is purely brand strategy and TOV -- no logo construction, no color specs with codes, no typography specs with font names, no applications. This is a content/communications strategy document, not a visual brand guidelines document.
