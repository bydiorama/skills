## Brand Strategy

- **Positioning**: Corwin is a real estate developer that positions sustainability as its core differentiator. Tagline: "Thinking green. Building the greenest."
- **Secondary tagline variant**: "Carrying greens. Building the greenest." (used on merchandise).
- **Mission statement (from CEO quote)**: "Our company was built on the idea that real estate development can be done better. That sustainability is not just a nice buzzword but a necessity."
- **Heritage**: The brand name references Matthias Corvinus (the "Raven King"), with the logo symbol encoding a raven's beak in negative space within a square form.
- **Five Core Principles** -- each with a dedicated color, symbol, and label:
  1. **Healthy City** -- Calm Water (wave symbol)
  2. **Green City** -- Grayish Green (upward arrows/trees symbol)
  3. **Compact City** -- Sunset Orange (circle symbol)
  4. **Diverse City** -- Pleasure (diamond/star symbol)
  5. **Flexible City** -- The Presence (bidirectional arrows symbol)
- **Subbrands**: "Offices by Corwin", "people x Corwin", "planet x Corwin", plus a generic "something x Corwin" template for future extensions.
- **Known projects**: PALMA ("People first district"), VILHARIA, Ein Park Offices, GUTHAUS, Kvartet, Lohkarton Kvart, Kalinka, Masarykova.
- **Partner collaborations**: Gehl, AllesWirdGut, Cube, 3FIS, Snohetta/JO, Softer.
- **Company entity**: Corwin SK a.s., Namestie Mateja Korvina 1, 811 07 Bratislava.
- **Website**: corwin.sk

## Logo Guidelines

### Primary Logo (Horizontal)
- **Composition**: Square icon (raven beak in negative space) + "CORWIN" wordmark in Circular Bold, all caps.
- **Typeface in logo**: Circular by Laurenz Brunner.
- **Construction**: Space between symbol and text = width of the letter "I" in CORWIN.
- **Safe zone**: At least 2x the width of the letter "I" on all sides.
- **Minimum size**: 20 mm (horizontal).
- **Color versions**: Two only -- Forest Green Dark (#1C4734) and White (#FFFFFF). No other colors permitted.
- **Contrast rule**: Green logo on light backgrounds; white logo on dark or photographed backgrounds.

### Vertical Logo (Stacked)
- Icon centered above "CORWIN" wordmark.
- Gap between symbol and text = half the height of the text part.
- Safe zone = height of the letter "I" on all sides.
- Minimum size: 9 mm.

### Favicon / Symbol Only
- Square icon used alone when space is severely limited (e.g., favicon, app icon).

### Versions to Avoid
- No proportion changes, no rotation, no recoloring.
- No placement on low-contrast backgrounds.
- No placement directly on busy photography without sufficient overlay/contrast.

### Subbrand Lockups
- **"Offices by Corwin"**: "Offices by" in lighter weight above "CORWIN" beside the icon. Same safezone/construction rules.
- **"something x Corwin"**: Uses a geometric X symbol (made of arrows) between the two names. Spacing = 2x the gap between symbol and text on each side of the X.
- **"Corwin | Something"** (co-branding with projects): Vertical divider line. Spacing between logos = 4x the gap between Corwin symbol and text. Partner logo must be proportionally balanced.
- **Multi-logo composition**: Primary logo at larger scale (top-left preferred), secondary logo 1/2x to 1/6x the height of the primary. Three approved positioning options for secondary logo.

## Typography

### Primary Font: Circular
- **Designer**: Laurenz Brunner (Swiss).
- **Weights used**: Regular, Medium, Bold.
- **Character**: Clean lines, modern geometric sans-serif. Used across all primary communications.

### Secondary / Fallback Font: Poppins
- **Weight used**: Medium.
- **Use case**: When Circular is unavailable (e.g., web fallback, third-party platforms).
- **Rationale**: Complements Circular with similar clean, geometric lines.

### Typographic Hierarchy (identical scale for both fonts)

| Level        | Size  | Line Height |
| ------------ | ----- | ----------- |
| Headline     | 300%  | 0.9         |
| Subheadline  | 180%  | 1.0         |
| Copy Large   | 120%  | 1.1         |
| Copy Medium  | 100%  | 1.2         |
| Copy Small   | 80%   | 1.3         |

- Sizes are relative/proportional (percentage-based), not fixed point sizes.
- Layout spacing uses a multiplier system (1x, 2x, 3x, 4x) for consistent element positioning.

## Supporting Graphics

### Principle Symbols (Pictograms)
Six custom geometric symbols, each representing a brand value:

1. **X-cross** -- "people x Corwin" identity mark (used in subbrand lockups)
2. **Waves / Zigzag chevrons** -- Healthy City
3. **Upward arrows / Trees** -- Green City
4. **Circle (ring)** -- Compact City
5. **Diamond / Rotated square star** -- Diverse City
6. **Bidirectional horizontal arrows** -- Flexible City

**Color usage**:
- White on Forest Green Dark (primary usage).
- Forest Green Bright on Forest Green Dark (tonal/monochromatic).
- Complementary tint on matching secondary color background (category usage).
- Do NOT use complementary color symbols on mismatched secondary backgrounds.

### Chevron Wave Pattern
- **Origin**: Derived from the Healthy City wave symbol.
- **Construction**: Built on a square grid; each grid cell contains two diagonal lines forming a chevron segment.
- **Minimum**: 4 diagonal lines. Can expand to 6 for more pronounced effect.
- **Colors**: Forest Green Bright on Forest Green Dark (two-tone green).
- **Formats**: Works across 1:1, 9:16, 16:9 ratios. Must always align to grid.
- **Positioning**: Must align with established grid system across all media.
- **Restrictions**: No isolated individual chevrons; no incomplete patterns; no arbitrary angles (must follow grid).

### Diagonal Overlay
- A distinctive diagonal cut (derived from chevron geometry) is used to separate photo and color/text areas in compositions. Appears consistently across print, digital, and billboard layouts.

## Photography

### Four Image Categories
1. **Projects**: Architectural photography of Corwin developments -- modern buildings with green facades, geometric patterns, urban integration with landscape.
2. **Internal**: Company culture -- office spaces, meetings, collaborative moments. Modern, artsy environments.
3. **Community**: Events and gatherings -- candid, genuine interactions. Authentic and unposed.
4. **Illustrative**: Nature close-ups -- leaves, petals, seeds, organic textures. Visual metaphor for sustainability values.

### Color Guidelines for Photography
- **Recommended palette**: Earthy tones -- blacks, deep greens, grays, muted browns, soft oranges, warm neutrals.
- **Avoid**: Bright, highly saturated colors (reds, neon greens, cyans, magentas, yellows, pinks, purples, vivid blues).

### Style to Avoid
- No stock-feel generic imagery.
- No staged group portraits.
- No bright/vivid sunsets or oversaturated landscapes.
- No food photography.
- No images with clashing non-brand color palettes.

## Tone of Voice

- **Not explicitly defined as a separate section**, but tone is inferred consistently from copy examples throughout:
  - **Confident and purposeful**: "Thinking green. Building the greenest." -- superlative claim, simple structure.
  - **Direct and concise**: Short declarative sentences. Maximum 3 lines for social media headlines.
  - **Sustainability-forward**: Every message connects back to environmental responsibility.
  - **Playful when appropriate**: "Carrying greens. Building the greenest." on tote bag.
  - **Bilingual**: Primary communications in English; local applications in Slovak.
  - **Professional but warm**: CEO letter uses first-person, values-driven language.

## Outliers / Notable

1. **Exceptionally comprehensive subbrand system**: The manual dedicates 10 full pages (pp. 16-24) to subbrand lockup rules (Offices by, x Corwin, Corwin | Partner), with precise mathematical spacing ratios. This level of subbrand architecture is unusual for a company of this type.

2. **Five-principle symbolic framework**: The entire visual identity is structured around 5 city-building principles, each with its own color, symbol, and nomenclature. This creates a rich but complex system -- potentially difficult to maintain consistency across teams.

3. **Kraft paper as brand material**: Stationery consistently uses natural/kraft paper stock rather than white, reinforcing sustainability positioning through material choice. This is a deliberate tactile brand decision.

4. **Urban honey as branded product**: "MESTSKY MED" (Urban Honey) as a merchandise item signals genuine community engagement beyond typical corporate swag.

5. **No explicit tone-of-voice section**: Despite 108 pages, the manual lacks a dedicated verbal identity/tone-of-voice chapter. Copy direction must be inferred from examples.

6. **No web/UI design section**: The digital section covers only slide decks and social media posts. No website design, app UI, or digital product guidelines are included.

7. **Diagonal chevron cut as compositional device**: The angled line separating photo from color block is a distinctive visual motif that appears across nearly every application. It is derived from the wave pattern geometry but used as a layout tool rather than just a decorative element.

8. **Circular font is premium/licensed**: The primary typeface (Circular by Lineto) is a commercial font requiring licensing, which explains why Poppins (free Google Font) is designated as the fallback.

9. **Landscape presentation format**: The entire 108-page manual is formatted at 1920x1080 (16:9), suggesting it was designed primarily for on-screen viewing/presentation rather than print.

## Quality Score

**4 out of 5**

**Strengths**:
- Extremely thorough logo guidelines with mathematical construction ratios and extensive subbrand system.
- Complete color specification (HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone, RAL for every color).
- Rich application section with 50+ pages of realistic, high-quality mockups covering print, digital, stationery, and merchandise.
- Coherent visual system tying five core principles to colors, symbols, and category branding.
- Strong contrast and "what to avoid" guidance with clear red-crossed examples throughout.

**Weaknesses**:
- No dedicated tone-of-voice / verbal identity section.
- No web/UI design or digital product guidelines.
- Typography section is brief (4 pages) -- no guidance on text color, paragraph spacing, or responsive sizing.
- No accessibility guidelines despite showing contrast considerations.
- Missing icon/illustration style guide beyond the 6 principle symbols.
