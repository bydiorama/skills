## Logo Guidelines

### Wordmark Construction
- The logo is a custom **all-caps wordmark**: **TECHBOX**.
- Set in **Sequel 100 Black /95** -- a geometric, ultra-bold sans-serif with an italic (forward-leaning) optical axis.
- Distinctive character-level details:
  - **T** -- slightly truncated crossbar on the left side, creating asymmetry.
  - **E** -- squared, industrial form with equal-length horizontal strokes.
  - **C** -- wide aperture, almost closing into an O.
  - **H** -- standard geometric construction.
  - **B** -- closed counters with subtle weight distribution.
  - **O** -- perfect circular counter with a small notch/cut at the upper-right, a signature brand detail.
  - **X** -- has a distinctive diamond-shaped negative space at the center where the strokes cross; the lower-right stroke appears slightly clipped, creating a dynamic, forward-motion feel.
- The overall wordmark conveys **speed, boldness, and forward momentum** through its italic lean and heavy weight.

### Logo Variants Shown
1. **White on black** (primary usage, page 2) -- dark background is the preferred placement.
2. **Black on light grey** (#F0F2F5 background, page 3) -- light-background variant.
3. **Black on each brand color** (page 4) -- the logo is always rendered in black on colored backgrounds, never reversed to white on color.
4. **White on black** appears again on cover (page 1) and closing page (page 7), reinforcing its primacy.

### Usage Rules
- No explicit clear-space rules, minimum-size specifications, or misuse examples are provided in this quickstart guide.
- No lockup with a tagline or icon is shown -- the wordmark operates as a standalone element.
- The logo is always shown at a single, consistent italic angle with no variation.

## Typography

Page 5 ("Logo and Typography") defines a clear four-tier type hierarchy:

| Role       | Typeface               | Weight   | Usage Notes                                           |
| ---------- | ---------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Logo       | Sequel 100 Black /95   | Black    | Used exclusively for the TECHBOX wordmark             |
| Headline   | Qanelas                | Bold     | Primary headline typeface; large-scale editorial use  |
| Overline   | Space Grotesk          | Bold     | All-caps spaced overline labels (e.g. "NEPREHLIADNITE")|
| Paragraph  | Acumin                 | Regular  | Body copy; demonstrated with a multi-line news excerpt |

### Observations
- **Sequel 100 Black** is a commercial display typeface by OGJ Type Design; its italic cut at 95-degree axis gives the logo its kinetic character.
- **Qanelas** is a geometric sans-serif (similar to Gilroy/Poppins family) used at large scale for article headlines.
- **Space Grotesk** serves the navigational/utility tier -- spaced-out uppercase labels, category tags, and UI elements.
- **Acumin** (Adobe) is a neutral neo-grotesque for body text, ensuring readability in long-form editorial content.
- The type system moves from expressive (logo) to neutral (body), establishing a clean editorial hierarchy.

## Outliers / Notable

1. **Extremely condensed scope.** At 7 pages, this is a quickstart reference rather than a comprehensive brand manual. It covers only logo, colors, type, and one application example. Critical sections like clear space, minimum sizes, misuse examples, iconography, and brand voice are absent.
2. **The "O" notch detail.** The small cut/notch in the upper-right of the "O" letterform is a subtle but distinctive brand signature that differentiates the wordmark from a simple type treatment.
3. **No icon or monogram.** The brand operates solely as a wordmark with no accompanying symbol, favicon-ready mark, or shorthand identifier -- a potential gap for digital/social applications.
4. **Slovak market focus.** All sample content is in Slovak, and the domain techbox.sk confirms this is a regional (Slovak) technology media brand.
5. **B&W photography convention.** The consistent use of monochrome photography in the application mockup is a strong editorial choice that deserves formal codification in a full brand guide.
6. **Color-as-category potential.** The 6 accent colors strongly suggest a content-category coding system, but this mapping is not documented.
7. **Sequel 100 Black licensing.** The logo typeface is a commercial font (OGJ Type Design), which may have licensing implications for any future brand system work.
