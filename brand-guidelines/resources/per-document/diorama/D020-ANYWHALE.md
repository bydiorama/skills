## Brand Strategy
- Cover page (p1): Large bold yellow "ANYWHALE" wordmark on cerulean/teal blue background. A whale tail icon sits directly beneath the "W", integrated into the logotype as a signature graphic element.
- Visual identity first impression: Playful, bold, high-contrast (yellow + blue). Rounded heavy typeface suggests approachability and energy.
- Concept (p6): The whale tail "fin slap" is the core brand metaphor. Text states: "This signature fin slap becomes our inspiration -- a bold, joyful gesture we borrow to craft a distinctive brand symbol. It flows through the entire Anywhale identity, anchoring our visual language with energy and play."
- Brand personality keywords implied: bold, joyful, energetic, playful, distinctive.
