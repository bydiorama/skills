# Brand Workshop Structure

## Plus/Minus/Interesting
The PMI method (Plus, Minus, Interesting) helps us quickly identify what makes us interesting, our strengths, and also highlights any weaknesses. It aids in aligning our thoughts based on initial associations.

## Rhetorical Triangle
The Rhetorical Triangle offers 3 pillars, of which utilizing at least 2 helps build trust with the audience. The model is proven, undoubtedly, since Aristotle introduced it to the world more than 2300 years ago.

We gradually associate ideas, thoughts, and concepts with each pillar.

**Ethos:** Trustworthiness, credibility, reliability
**Pathos:** Emotional appeal, empathy, connection, expectation
**Logos:** Logical reasoning, evidence, facts
**Overlap:** Brand Trust

## Brand Personality
Our brand has a personality, and if it seems otherwise, it may just be conservative and minimalist, or perhaps so intertwined with our identity that we overlook its individual character. 

The goal of the exercise is to define who we are based on fundamental pairs of traits – and to exclude who we are not.

## CEPs Canvas
In which specific situations should the market think about us?

1. **Why?** - Why are they buying - motives and benefits e.g. to get
2. **How?** - How can we help them - solutions and services e.g. to do
3. **When?** - When should they buy - timing and urgency e.g. to buy now
4. **Where?** - Where should they buy - location and convenience e.g. online or offline
5. **Who?** - Who should they buy from - competitors and partners e.g. to buy from us
6. **What?** - What should they buy - products and services e.g. to buy this product
7. **While?** - While they buy - co-activities e.g. while in a meeting
8. **With whom?** - With/for whom do they buy - other people e.g. broad would approve

## A Look into the Future
Where do you see yourself in 5 years? Probably everyone hates this question. However, looking into the future is responsible and helps us change our perspective on who we are.

If the media were to write about us at that time - what would they say, what would the headline be?
